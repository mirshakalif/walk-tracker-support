# Walk Tracker Support

Walk Tracker records your walks and runs on Apple Watch, and shows their history on your watch
and iPhone.

## Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: [mirshakalif@outlook.com](mailto:mirshakalif@outlook.com)

## Common questions

**A workout isn't showing on my iPhone or watch.** Workouts arrive after you finish them on your
watch, once the watch and iPhone are connected, and the two keep the same History: a walk on one
goes to the other, and one you delete on either goes from both. After you install the watch app
again, your walks come back from the iPhone. In the iPhone app, tap ••• and then Sync History
with Watch to compare them now.

**How do I save a workout to Apple Health?** Switch Apple Health on in the summary before you tap
Done, or open the workout from History later and tap Add to Apple Health.

**Can I see miles instead of kilometres?** Yes. In Settings, on your watch or iPhone, set Distance
to Miles. Distances, speed, pace, splits and route markers all follow, and past workouts too.

**How do I use the heart rate from my Whoop or a chest strap?** On a Whoop, first turn on Heart
Rate Broadcast in the Whoop app. Then on your Apple Watch open Walk Tracker, go to Settings, and
under Heart rate from choose Watch + sensor or Sensor only, and pick it. During a workout a small
radio-waves mark beside the heart rate shows it's coming from the sensor. With Watch + sensor,
the watch's own heart rate takes over while the sensor is out of reach; with Sensor only, the
heart rate waits for it. Choose Off for no heart rate in Walk Tracker. Workouts saved to Apple
Health keep the watch's own.

**How do I note something during a walk?** During a workout, swipe right from the main screen:
the note starts recording straight away, so just say it; it stops after a few seconds of quiet,
or swipe back, and the walk carries on. Or say "Hey Siri, note in Walk Tracker". To start a note
with double tap, set Settings › Notes › Double tap to Note. To talk into the headphones connected
to your iPhone instead, choose Settings › Notes › Microphone › Headphones. On your iPhone, every
note is under Notes on History. Notes are on the walk's page in History, with
numbered pins on its route. Your iPhone writes voice notes out as text the next time you open
Walk Tracker on it. Share all (or touch and hold a note) sends just the words, the words with
the time and distance of each, or the voice recordings themselves.

**Can I add the song I'm hearing to my Favourites?** Yes, if it's Apple Music playing on your
iPhone. During a workout, turn the Digital Crown to Music and tap the star in the top-left
corner. The first time, open Walk Tracker on your iPhone and allow Apple Music. Songs from
other apps, or played from the watch itself, can't be added.

**Can I skip the Pace & Effort page?** Yes. Switch Settings › Workout screens › Pace & Effort
page off, on your watch or iPhone, and one swipe from the main screen takes you to Music.

**How do I hear how I'm doing?** Set Settings › Notes or Alerts › Double tap to Update and double
tap during a workout, or say "Hey Siri, how am I doing in Walk Tracker?". Settings › Alerts on
your iPhone chooses what each alert says and when, and warns you when your pace, heart rate or
cadence is off. Settings › Vibrations lets you feel each alert's tap, switch any off or make
them stronger.

**How do I add Walk Tracker to my watch face?** Open Walk Tracker on your watch once. Then touch
and hold the watch face, tap Edit, swipe to the complications, tap a spot and choose Walk
Tracker: This Week shows this week's distance, and Walk Tracker shows the walking figure. On
your iPhone you can do the same in the Watch app, under My Faces.

**Can I use Walk Tracker on a treadmill?** Yes. Choose Indoor on the Start screen before you
start. Your watch works out the distance from your arm movement and stride; afterwards, tap the
pencil next to the distance to correct it to what the treadmill showed.

**How do I race my usual route?** Save it first: on your iPhone, open a walk on that route in
History and tap Save as route, or plan one under Routes. Your iPhone sends your routes to your
watch. When a walk follows one, after the first half kilometre your watch asks "Park loop?" (the
route's name); tap Race it and each split shows how far ahead or behind your best you are.
Settings › Routes chooses whether it asks, races without asking or never does, and whether you
race your best, your average or your last time.

**Can I follow or start a walk from my iPhone?** Yes. While a walk is going on your watch, open
Walk Tracker on your iPhone, or look at the Lock Screen, to see it live and pause or end it. To
start one, tap Start a walk on your watch on History, with your watch on your wrist and
unlocked. The first time, allow Health for Walk Tracker on your iPhone.

**How do I start music with my walk?** In Settings › Music, set Music at start to Carry on (what
was last playing) or Playlist, and pick one. It plays through the Music app on your iPhone.
Settings › Music › Music page chooses Walk Tracker's own page, with a big cover, or Apple's.

**Can I set my own heart rate zones?** Yes. In Settings › Heart rate, choose My own and set
where each zone starts. Show the zone's range puts it beside the zone during a workout.

**Can I change the start sound?** Yes, in Settings › Start: a whistle, "Let's go", a bell, a horn,
beeps, just a tap, or none, and the LET'S GO animation on or off.
