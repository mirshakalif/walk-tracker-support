# Walk Tracker Support

Walk Tracker records your walks and runs on Apple Watch, and shows their history on your watch
and iPhone.

## Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: [mirshakalif@outlook.com](mailto:mirshakalif@outlook.com)

## Common questions

**A workout isn't showing on my iPhone.** Workouts arrive after you finish them on your watch,
once the watch and iPhone are connected. In the iPhone app, tap ••• and then Get All Workouts
from Watch to fetch everything again.

**How do I save a workout to Apple Health?** Switch Apple Health on in the summary before you tap
Done, or open the workout from History later and tap Add to Apple Health.
