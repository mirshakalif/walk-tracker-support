# Walk Tracker Support

Walk Tracker records your walks and runs on Apple Watch, and shows their history on your watch
and iPhone.

## Common questions

**A workout isn't showing on my iPhone.** Workouts arrive after you finish them on your watch,
once the watch and iPhone are connected. In the iPhone app, tap ••• and then Get All Workouts
from Watch to fetch everything again.

**How do I save a workout to Apple Health?** Switch Apple Health on in the summary before you tap
Done, or open the workout from History later and tap Add to Apple Health.

**Can I see miles instead of kilometres?** Yes. In Settings, on your watch or iPhone, set Distance
to Miles. Distances, speed, pace, splits and route markers all follow, and past workouts too.

**How do I use the heart rate from my Whoop or a chest strap?** On a Whoop, first turn on Heart
Rate Broadcast in the Whoop app. Then on your Apple Watch open Walk Tracker, go to Settings, tap
Sensor under Heart rate from, and pick it. During a workout a small radio-waves mark beside the
heart rate shows it's coming from the sensor. If the sensor goes out of reach, the watch's own
heart rate takes over until it's back. Workouts saved to Apple Health keep the watch's own.
