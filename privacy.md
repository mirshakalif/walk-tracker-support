# Walk Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Walk Tracker records your walks and runs on your Apple Watch and shows their history on your
watch and iPhone. This policy explains what the app uses and where it goes.

## The short version

- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- Your workouts are stored on your own Apple Watch and iPhone. We never receive them.
- Nothing is shared with third parties.

## What the app uses, and why

**Workouts.** Each workout's time, distance, steps, pace, heart rate, calories, splits and route
are stored in the app on your Apple Watch, and copied to Walk Tracker on your paired iPhone so you
can see your history there. The copy travels directly between your devices using Apple's
WatchConnectivity, not through any server of ours.

**Apple Health.** With your permission, Walk Tracker reads your heart rate during workouts, and can
read your date of birth, sex and weight to set heart rate zones and estimate calories. It saves a
workout, with its route, to Apple Health only when you switch Apple Health on for that workout.
Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared.

**Location.** GPS is used during a workout to measure distance, record splits and draw the route.
It isn't used at any other time.

**Motion.** The motion sensors count your steps and cadence during a workout.

**Settings.** Your goals and preferences are stored in the app on your devices.

## Your choices

You can turn off Health, Location or Motion access at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone
or Apple Watch. Deleting Walk Tracker from a device deletes its workout history from that device.
Workouts you saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

## Children

Walk Tracker doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
